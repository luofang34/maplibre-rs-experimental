The bundled innsbruck-approach.json is a derivative database of 55 observed ADS-B positions.
Its source checksum, coverage, and altitude conversion are included in that file.
No landing is invented. Replay ends at the end of available receiver coverage.
Altitude conversion grid: https://cdn.proj.org/us_nga_egm96_15.tif (NGA EGM96, public domain).
Trace format: https://github.com/wiedehopf/readsb/blob/dev/README-json.md

Desk globe overview
Natural Earth 1:50m land, public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Rendered by maplibre-rs, then downsampled to a bounded 2048 x 2048 overview texture.
Detailed terrain is rendered separately by maplibre-rs in the immersive view.

Conquering the Mach Loop
Original simulated flight, not a recorded ADS-B track or operational route.
Location reference: https://www.mjaviation.co.uk/MachLoop.htm
Terrain: AWS Terrarium / Tilezen, https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
The generator and source checksums are included with this application.

SVS design reference

The included PDF is a reference document. The application does not claim certification.
