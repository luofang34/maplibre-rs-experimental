Module celestial 

Source
Expand description

Sun and sky inputs a host supplies in Earth-centred, Earth-fixed axes.

The renderer computes no astronomy: a host works out where the sun and the stars are and hands their directions over as EcefDirections. While no sun is set, the style’s root light drives the atmosphere as it does without this module.

The Moon’s light on the night side: its direction in globe world axes and, as w, the light it adds overhead: MOONLIGHT_GAIN times its lit fraction (1 − cos elongation) / 2, from the angle between the Sun and Moon directions, times the share of sunlight the Earth’s shadow lets reach the Moon’s centre. All zero without both bodies.

set_sun_visible_fraction
Sets the share of the sun’s disc left uncovered where the eye stands. The sky’s scattered sunlight and the day side’s light scale with it; the sun’s disc and its glare do not, as the moon’s disc covers the one and the other stands in for the corona. The ground has a day side only under a host’s sun direction, so without one only the sky dims. The share is the eye’s alone and the whole day side takes it, so it suits a view from the ground; a view of the globe leaves it at 1.
