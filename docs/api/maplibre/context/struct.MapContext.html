Mutable frame state shared by scheduled systems, independent of host service types.

pub fn fly_to(&mut self, options: CameraOptions, curve: FlightCurve) -> FlightId

Moves the camera to options along curve, as GL JS easeTo and flyTo do, from the next frame on, in either navigation mode; a move under way is interrupted. The center’s height eases from where it starts to the height the destination rests at: the elevation options gives, else, where terrain holds the center, the ground loaded there, sea level until it loads, as Self::jump_to stands a center. Moving the camera any other way interrupts the move.

pub fn loaded_ground_cached(&self, position: LatLon) -> Option<f64>

Ground height in metres at a location from the finest DEM tile loaded so far, drawn or not; None without terrain or before any tile covering the location has arrived. The answer improves as finer tiles load, so read it again rather than keeping it.

Source

pub fn drawn_ground_cached( &self, position: LatLon, ) -> Result<Option<f64>, TerrainUnknown>

Height of the ground the last frame drew at a location, for converting between the screen and the ground: Ok(None) without terrain or outside the drawn tiles, and TerrainUnknown over a drawn tile whose DEM has not loaded. A DEM loaded elsewhere in the cache does not stand in for it.

Source

pub fn drawn_terrain(&self, body: Body) -> Option<DrawnTerrain<'_>>

The terrain the last frame drew, for following lines of sight across it with target_view; None without terrain or before any of it is drawn. body is the camera’s (GlobeCameraState::body).

pub fn set_sun_direction(&mut self, sun: Option<EcefDirection>)

Sets the direction towards the sun in ECEF axes, which drives the atmosphere in place of the style’s root light and darkens the terrain’s night side; None returns the atmosphere to the style’s light and lights all terrain as day.

Source

Source

pub fn set_atmosphere(&mut self, atmosphere: bool)

Draws the globe’s atmosphere in full, lit by the sun direction while one is set, even when the style has no sky; false leaves the atmosphere to the style’s sky.

Source

Source

pub fn set_sun_angular_radius(&mut self, radius: Option<AngularRadius>)

Sets or clears the sun’s angular radius. The disc stands where Self::set_sun_direction points, with a glare that follows how much of it shows; with no sun direction it is not drawn. The starfield plugin draws it.

Source

pub fn set_moon_disc(&mut self, moon: Option<Disc>)

Sets or clears the moon’s disc. Its direction is the one seen from the camera’s eye: the moon is near enough that the Earth’s centre sees it elsewhere. Its phase follows Self::set_sun_direction; with no sun set, the whole disc is drawn lit, a fallback rather than a phase. The starfield plugin draws it.

Source

pub fn set_earth_shadow(&mut self, shadow: Option<EarthShadow>)

Sets or clears the Earth’s shadow the moon is lit through during a lunar eclipse, as the host computes it: the moon dims and turns copper in the umbra, as dark as the shadow’s Danjon value, and its light on the night side dims with it.

Source

pub fn sun_direction(&self) -> Option<EcefDirection>

The sun direction set with Self::set_sun_direction.

pub fn set_navigation_mode( &mut self, mode: NavigationMode, ) -> Result<(), NavigationError>

Switches how the camera navigates the globe, for the style’s projection: free-globe navigation needs the pure vertical-perspective projection. A center held by terrain stands on the loaded ground under it at once, as crate::terrain::elevation::ground_center stands it.

Source

pub fn jump_to(&mut self, options: CameraOptions) -> Result<(), NavigationError>

Moves the map at once, as GL JS jumpTo, in either navigation mode, as ViewState::jump_to moves it. A center held by terrain stands on the loaded ground under it at once, or at sea level where none has loaded, never on the ground it left.

Source

pub fn restore_globe_pose( &mut self, pose: GlobePose, ) -> Result<(), NavigationError>

Restores a stored free-globe pose, for the style’s projection: it must be the pure vertical-perspective projection. A center held by terrain stands on the loaded ground under the pose’s target at once, rather than at the pose’s own target height, as crate::terrain::elevation::ground_center stands it.

pub fn mutate_style( &mut self, apply: impl FnOnce(&mut Style) -> Result<StyleChange, StyleMutationError>, ) -> Result<StyleChange, StyleMutationError>

Applies a style change and fetches loaded vector tiles again when it needs that; the old content stays on screen until the new tiles arrive. GeoJSON data the change replaced, as with Style::set_geojson_data, refreshes the tiles it changed in by itself, so a caller that only replaces data returns StyleChange::default(): redraw_tiles would fetch every loaded tile of every source again.
