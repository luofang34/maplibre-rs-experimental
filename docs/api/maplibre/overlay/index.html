Module overlay 

Source
Expand description

Triangles a host draws over the map, inside the map’s own frame.

A host often draws content of its own over a map: lines and figures sized in screen pixels, edit handles, previews. Drawing them over the map’s finished image keeps them out of the map’s passes, so nothing the map draws can be ordered with them and every host needs a compositor. A host instead hands the map one OverlayMesh per frame with the headless map’s set_overlay: triangles in the render target’s pixels, drawn over the map’s layers and under its symbols in the order given, so a host that lists every casing before any line gets casings under all of its lines, and the map’s labels stay readable over them.

Each vertex carries the depth of the ground it draws, and the pass tests it against the frame’s depth as the map’s own 3D geometry is tested, so terrain hides overlay content behind it at no cost per vertex.

Content a host draws over the map’s finished image instead, such as text, can be tested the same way with DepthProbes, answered a frame or two later.

Whether a point is hidden is decided by its line of sight across the drawn terrain, target_view; the depth test here is a cheap stand-in for it, and differs from it in two ways only. It lets through ground less than DEPTH_BIAS of its depth behind nearer ground, which the line of sight calls hidden. And it compares a point with the nearest ground the frame drew anywhere in its pixel, at every sample of a multisampled frame, so a point beside a ridge’s silhouette, or on ground seen at a grazing angle, can be hidden by ground in its pixel that does not lie on its own line. Over hills at 80° of pitch the two disagree for about 1 % of ground points.

OverlayPlugin adds the passes; a map without it ignores the mesh and the probes.

Constants§

DEPTH_BIAS
Share by which a vertex’s or probe’s reversed-Z depth is raised before it is tested against the frame’s depth. That brings it nearer by about DEPTH_BIAS * (1 - distance / far) of its distance from the camera: the full share close to the camera, less towards the far plane. Overlay lines are straight chords between points on the ground, a few pixels apart, so over a convex slope they run a little under the drawn surface; this keeps them from hiding what lies on the ground, while a ridge hides what lies behind it by far more.
DEPTH_PROBE_PASS
The probe pass’s node in the draw graph, after the overlay pass.
MAX_PROBES
Probes answered at once; more are left unanswered.
OVERLAY_PASS
