Module sun 

Source
Expand description

The sun’s disc and its glare.

The disc is limb-darkened and lies at the far plane, tested against depth without writing it, so the ground, terrain and every layer cover the part of it below their horizon. The glare around it brightens with the share of the disc the ground leaves showing: the vertex shader samples the depth the main pass left, copied by depth::SunDepthCopyNode, across the disc. Neither fades with the sky the stars fade with: the sun is what lights it.
