Module moon 

Source
Expand description

The moon’s disc, lit on the side the sun direction shows it.

The phase is geometry only: each pixel of the disc stands for a point on a sphere, lit where its normal faces the sun direction, so the lit share and the bright limb’s orientation both follow from the sun and moon directions. The dark side keeps a faint earthshine. With no sun direction set the whole disc is drawn lit, a fallback rather than a phase. The disc lies at the far plane like the sun’s, and does not fade with the sky: the moon shows by day.
