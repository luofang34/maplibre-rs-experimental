The day and night of a globe drawn without terrain, under the host’s sun.

Terrain turns its drape from day to night as it draws it. Without terrain every layer lies on the globe itself, so one pass after the main pass multiplies what it drew by the share of its colour each point of the globe keeps, as crate::celestial::TwilightBand and the share of the sun an eclipse leaves set it. The pass runs before the translucent pass, so symbols, the sun, the moon and the atmosphere keep their own light, as they do over terrain. It shades only where the main pass wrote depth: the globe’s background and anything standing on it, not the sky or the stars behind the globe.
