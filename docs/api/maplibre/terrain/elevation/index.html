The height of the point the camera orbits at the map center, from the loaded DEM tiles.

Functions§

center_elevation_system
Lifts the camera’s orbit point onto the terrain under the map center every frame, and records the lowest elevation of the tile under it for the far plane.
center_ground 🔒
The loaded ground under the map center the center stands on, or None where it is not loaded: the DEM tile at the integer zoom, as GL JS lifts its center by, not the tile drawn under it, which a coarser level may stand in for at a distance.
center_target_system
Decides what the camera orbits at the center every frame, terrain or not: whether the globe camera orbits the center’s elevation, and, without terrain or with the center off the ground, rests the center at its altitude. A gesture holds it where it is.
globe_orbits_center 🔒
Whether the globe camera of style orbits the center’s elevation.
ground_center
Stands the center on the loaded ground under it, where terrain holds the center and no gesture freezes it, as the frame does; a host placing the camera between frames calls this so what it reads back already stands on the ground, as GL JS jumpTo grounds its center. Returns whether the center now stands on the ground; where the ground under it is not loaded, its elevation stays as it was.
past_mercator_rows 🔒
Whether a free-globe camera looks past the last row of tiles, where the cap closes the globe at sea level; the flat center stops at that row.
