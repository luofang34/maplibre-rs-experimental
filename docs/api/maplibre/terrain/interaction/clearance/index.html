maplibre::terrain::interaction

Module clearance 

Source
Expand description

Enforces camera eye and perspective near-plane clearance above 3D terrain.

Sampling a single point at (eye.x, eye.y) with zero vertical margin allows the perspective near clip quad (near_z = height / 50) to slice triangular holes into steep 60°–75° Alpine ridges (such as Nordkette above Innsbruck or the Matterhorn) and traps constant-center orbit cameras when min_zoom or max_pitch clamps a single-step lift. Here a 5-point footprint spanning the eye and the 4 horizontal corners of the near clip quad is checked against both the finest loaded DEM and the stitched rendered surface, with a positive clearance scaled to the near-plane world radius.
