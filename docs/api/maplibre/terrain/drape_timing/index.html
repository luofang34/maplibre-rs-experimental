Module drape_timing

What redrawing a drape costs on the GPU, so a moving view draws only what fits its frame.

Zooming can invalidate a whole batch of drapes at once; redrawing all of them in one frame stalls that frame and every host waiting on it. The drape passes are timed together with timestamp queries where the device has them, and the cost of one drape is a running average of those measurements. While the camera moves, a frame draws as many drapes as its time budget holds; the rest keep their previous texture or show an ancestor’s, and are drawn once the view settles.
