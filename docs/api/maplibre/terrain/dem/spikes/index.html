maplibre::terrain::dem

Module spikes 

Source
Expand description

Removal of the narrow elevation spikes that some DEM mosaics carry along source seams.

Resampling across a seam leaves runs of one or two samples hundreds of metres above both neighbours. Bare-earth terrain is not that steep on both flanks, so a sample that rises above the median of its row or column window by more than SPIKE_SLOPE ground sample distances takes that median. Coarse tiles, where one sample can hold a real isolated peak, have a threshold of kilometres and keep their peaks. Narrow pits are kept: a gorge one sample wide is real terrain, and the seams’ shallow dips do not stand out against the horizon.

Constants§

MAX_ENCODED 🔒
Encoded value range of the three 8-bit channels.
SPIKE_SLOPE 🔒
Policy: how many ground sample distances a sample may rise above its window median before it counts as a spike. Seam ringing is one or two samples wide and stands hundreds of metres proud. For terrain to rise three sample distances above the median of its five-sample window, it must climb steeper than atan(3), about 72°, on both flanks. Bare-earth DEMs average the ground over each sample and hold no such slopes, so steep real relief stays untouched.
WINDOW_RADIUS 🔒
Samples on each side of a sample in its row and column windows. Two lets a spike two samples wide stay a minority of its window.
